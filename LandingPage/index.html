PROTOCOL SUITE

50+ Live-Tracked Exercises

From bench press to Bulgarian split squats, planks to pull-ups — each movement has its own joint-angle checkpoints, partial-rep detection and tempo checks. Here are five of the classics.

🦵 PROTOCOL 01

Squats

Target: Quadriceps & Gluteus Maximus
  • Knee Flexion Angle (<90° for full depth)
  • Torso Inclination & Back Upright Angle
  • Femur-Parallel Crease Detection
  • Anti-Valgus Knee Cave Protection
TARGET DEPTH: 80° – 90°
💪 PROTOCOL 02

Push-ups

Target: Pectoralis Major & Core
  • Elbow Flexion Angle (~90° bottom turn)
  • Full Ear-to-Ankle Spine Alignment
  • Real-time Hip Sag / Arch Detection
  • Top Lockout Extension Check
TARGET INFLECTION: 85° – 90°
🏋️ PROTOCOL 03

Dumbbell Curls

Target: Biceps Brachii & Forearms
  • Full Peak Flexion (<45°)
  • Elbow Pinned To Ribcage Guard
  • Anti-Torso Momentum Swing Monitor
  • Eccentric Extension Lowering Rate
PEAK CONTRACTION: 35° – 45°
🔝 PROTOCOL 04

Shoulder Press

Target: Anterior Deltoids & Triceps
  • Vertical 180° Overhead Lockout
  • Elbow Descent to 90° Ear Level
  • Lumbar Hyperextension Shield
  • Forearm Plumb-Line Stability
OVERHEAD LOCKOUT: 170° – 180°
🏃 PROTOCOL 05

Walking Lunges

Target: Glutes, Quads & Stabilizers
  • Lead Knee 90° Square Angle Stride
  • Vertical Torso Posture Balance
  • Trailing Knee Floor Proximity
  • Pelvic Tilt & Anti-Sway Tracking
LEAD KNEE BEND: 88° – 92°
