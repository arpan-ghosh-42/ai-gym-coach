# Home Workout Programming

## Can you build muscle at home?
Yes. Studies show that push-ups performed close to failure produce similar chest and triceps hypertrophy to bench press at comparable effort (Kikuchi & Nakazato, 2017), and low-load training to near-failure builds muscle similar to heavy training (Schoenfeld et al., 2015). The key is taking sets close to failure (0-3 RIR) and progressing difficulty over time.

## Equipment tiers
- Bodyweight only: push-up progressions, squats, lunges, glute bridges, planks, pike push-ups, chair dips, towel or doorframe rows.
- Dumbbells (adjustable preferred): adds goblet squats, Romanian deadlifts, dumbbell rows, floor press, shoulder press, curls, lateral raises.
- Resistance bands: adds band rows, face pulls, pull-aparts, band-assisted pull-ups, banded lateral walks, triceps pushdowns.
- Pull-up bar: adds pull-ups, chin-ups, hanging knee raises - the best home back and biceps builder.

## Bodyweight progressions (easiest to hardest)
- Push: wall push-up -> incline push-up -> knee push-up -> push-up -> decline push-up -> deficit push-up -> archer push-up -> one-arm push-up negatives.
- Vertical push: pike push-up -> elevated pike push-up -> wall-assisted handstand hold -> handstand push-up negatives.
- Pull: towel door row -> table/inverted row -> band-assisted pull-up -> negative pull-up -> pull-up -> chin-up / weighted backpack pull-up.
- Squat: bodyweight squat -> tempo squat (3-1-1) -> split squat -> Bulgarian split squat -> shrimp squat -> pistol squat.
- Hinge: glute bridge -> single-leg glute bridge -> hip thrust off couch -> single-leg Romanian deadlift -> Nordic hamstring curl negatives.
- Core: plank -> side plank -> dead bug -> hollow body hold -> hanging knee raise.

## Making home training harder without heavier weights
Add reps; slow the lowering phase to 3-4 seconds; add a 1-2 second pause at the bottom; use 1.5 reps (half rep + full rep); reduce rest; use a backpack loaded with books; move to the next progression once you can do more than 20 clean reps.

## Home split recommendations
- 3 days: full body each session (squat pattern, push, pull, hinge, core).
- 4 days: upper/lower. Upper = push-ups, rows, pike push-ups, pull-ups or band rows, dips, curls. Lower = Bulgarian split squats, single-leg RDLs, glute bridges, lunges, calf raises, core.
- 5-6 days: push/pull/legs or upper/lower/full body, with sets taken close to failure and rep ranges of 10-25.
