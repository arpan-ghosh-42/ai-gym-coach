# Exercise Library by Muscle Group (Gym and Home)

## Chest
- Gym: barbell bench press (5-8 reps), incline dumbbell press (8-12), machine chest press (8-12), cable fly (12-15), weighted dips (6-10).
- Home: push-ups and progressions (10-25), dumbbell floor press (8-12), dumbbell fly on floor (12-15), chair dips (10-20).

## Back
- Gym: pull-ups / lat pulldown (6-12), barbell or chest-supported row (6-10), seated cable row (8-12), single-arm dumbbell row (8-12), face pulls (12-20).
- Home: pull-ups or band-assisted pull-ups, inverted table rows (8-15), single-arm dumbbell row (10-15), band rows (12-20), band pull-aparts (15-25).

## Shoulders
- Gym: barbell or dumbbell overhead/shoulder press (6-10), lateral raise (12-20), rear delt fly or face pull (12-20).
- Home: dumbbell shoulder press (8-12), pike push-up (8-15), dumbbell or band lateral raise (12-20), band face pull (15-20).

## Biceps
- Gym: barbell curl (8-12), dumbbell biceps curl (8-12), incline dumbbell curl (10-12), hammer curl (10-15), cable curl (12-15).
- Home: dumbbell biceps curl (8-15), hammer curl (10-15), chin-ups (6-12), band curl (12-20).

## Triceps
- Gym: close-grip bench press (6-10), cable pushdown (10-15), overhead cable or dumbbell extension (10-15), dips.
- Home: diamond push-up (8-20), chair dips (10-20), dumbbell overhead triceps extension (10-15), band pushdown (15-20).

## Quadriceps
- Gym: back squat (5-8), front squat (6-8), leg press (8-12), hack squat (8-12), leg extension (12-15), walking lunges (10-12 per leg).
- Home: bodyweight or goblet squats (10-20), Bulgarian split squats (8-15 per leg), lunges / reverse lunges (10-15 per leg), step-ups (10-15 per leg).

## Hamstrings and glutes
- Gym: Romanian deadlift (6-10), conventional deadlift (3-6), hip thrust (8-12), lying or seated leg curl (10-15), back extension (12-15).
- Home: dumbbell Romanian deadlift (10-15), single-leg Romanian deadlift (10-12 per leg), glute bridge / single-leg glute bridge (12-20), Nordic curl negatives (4-8), slider hamstring curl (10-15).

## Calves
- Gym: standing calf raise (10-15), seated calf raise (12-20).
- Home: single-leg calf raise on a step (12-20).

## Core
- Plank (30-60 s), side plank (20-45 s per side), dead bug (8-12 per side), hanging knee raise (10-15), cable crunch (12-15), ab wheel rollout (6-12).

## Pose Coach supported movements
The CoachX live Pose Coach currently detects and corrects: Squats (knee angle, back angle, depth), Push-ups (elbow angle, body alignment, hip sag), Biceps Curls with dumbbells (elbow flexion, shoulder stability, torso swing), Shoulder Press (lockout, elbow angle, back arch), and Lunges (front knee angle, torso angle, balance).
